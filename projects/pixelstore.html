02

Payments & Invoice

The server creates the payment order while the client opens Razorpay Checkout. Payment details are verified server-side before the order is marked as paid.

Once payment is confirmed, Pixel Store generates its own print-ready invoice from the completed order, including purchased items, pricing and payment details.

Pixel Store uses Razorpay's test environment only. No real money is involved.
